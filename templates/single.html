🩸 Blood Donation Awareness

Why Blood Donation is Important

Blood donation saves lives. Hospitals require blood for surgeries, accident victims, cancer patients, and many emergency treatments. A single donation can help up to three patients.

Who Can Donate Blood?

  • Age between 18 – 60 years
  • Minimum weight 50 kg
  • Healthy and free from infections
  • Minimum 90 days gap between donations

Blood Compatibility Chart

Blood Group Can Donate To
O- All Blood Groups
O+ O+, A+, B+, AB+
A+ A+, AB+
B+ B+, AB+
AB+ AB+ Only

Emergency Tips

  • Stay calm and contact nearby blood donors
  • Always keep emergency hospital numbers
  • Check blood compatibility before transfusion
